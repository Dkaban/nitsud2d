Nitsud2D • Getting started

Welcome to your own visual 2D game editor. This is the first playable alpha.

  • Select: click an object in the scene or hierarchy.
  • Move: drag an object; toggle Snap for grid alignment.
  • Place: pick an object from Add Object and click the world. Press Escape to stop placing.
  • Camera: mouse wheel to zoom; Space + drag or middle-mouse drag to pan; Fit resets view.
  • Quick actions: Delete to remove, Ctrl/Cmd+D to duplicate, Ctrl/Cmd+S to save.
  • Play: press Play and use WASD or arrow keys to move. Press Stop to edit again.
  • Projects: autosave locally; Export and Open move your scene as a JSON file.

Up next: animation timelines, tile painting, richer components and standalone game export.
